How this farm works

🟩

1. Build on land

Empty plots do nothing. Click an empty plot and choose to build a cow pen or a chicken coop on it.

🌽

2. Keep them fed

Animals only produce while their hunger bar is above zero. Buy feed from the shop and click "Feed" on a hungry animal.

🥛

3. Collect the goods

A fed cow slowly fills its trough with milk; a fed chicken fills its nest with eggs. Click "Collect" once the bar is full.

🧀

4. Age it up (optional)

Build the Creamery to turn raw milk into cheese over time. Cheese sells for a lot more than milk.

🪙

5. Sell & expand

Sell milk, eggs and cheese in the Market, then spend coins on more feed, new animals, or clearing more land to build on.

💾

Your progress saves itself

The farm autosaves in your browser every few seconds. Close the tab any time — it'll be exactly as you left it.
